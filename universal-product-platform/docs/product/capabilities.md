# Product Capabilities

The platform is designed to be reusable across e-commerce, fintech, healthcare, SaaS, education, travel, logistics, media, and automotive use cases.

Core capabilities include:

- User and identity management
- Product and catalog management
- Search
- Personalized recommendations
- Feed management
- Subscription management
- Payments and billing
- Orders
- Notifications
- Analytics
- Authentication and authorization
- Feature flags
- Observability
- API management
